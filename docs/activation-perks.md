# Third forty-perk batch

Exactly forty previously Unsupported source-card definitions now have calculations, activation previews, or selectable status sources. All forty remain Partial: this is a snapshot builder, and the complete proc timeline, healing, movement, summon behavior, and uncertain formulas are not simulated. No new runtime raw-data catalog was added.

Definitions remain in `src/data/Perks.ts`, `Buffs.ts`, and `Debuffs.ts`. The existing `Combat.ts` resolves damage; `Build.ts` applies selected status stats after perk stat conversions.

## Shared behavior and controls

Choose qualifying conditions from Buffs: Blocking, Stoneskin Active, Sharpshooter Range, Stealth Advantage, Maximum Voxos, Inventory Bonus Capped, Active Summons, Hammer Slam, and High Knockback. These are scenario controls and do not add game-buff stacks for Ork or Emotional. Channeled Depths is also excluded until its game classification is verified.

Luminescent Fervor, Queen’s Power, and Cleansed Blade carry their actual calculated behavior on the selected shared buff, so recipients do not need the original perk. Source potency is an application snapshot. Applicant stat/potency bonuses remain live; final manual effective potency overrides bypass source modifiers. Roaring Heads reduces received Weakness and Shatter only when automatic potency is used. Unmoving always forces Slow to 0.3, including overrides, matching its explicit source exception.

The Effect Added Scalings/Damage Types panels include both perk and selected-status effects. The Perk Activation Damage panel previews each separate activation and does not add those totals to normal weapon, Rune, or Weapon Art damage. Fiery Pursuit previews its documented floor(perk amount) dashes as one activation. Aura ticks, tornadoes, lightning, and shockwaves are individual activations rather than DPS or expected proc rates. Their required trigger is assumed unless an implemented snapshot condition (such as owned Poison or Weakness) is checked.

## Assumptions still requiring game verification

- Serrated Edge’s damage beyond one perk uses provisional linear stacking. The source explicitly gives only the one-perk damage; its additive Bleed potency scales per perk.
- Defensive Stance and Pain Distribution use the existing diminishing-return reduction convention. Their cards do not specify direct versus diminishing stacking.
- Gorecast applies its bonus to a qualifying hybrid packet as a whole. Elemental-only packets are excluded.
- Cleansed Blade reads the status holder’s Water Boost; transferred-buff scaling ownership is not established.
- Pure elemental output is inferred from the attack description when a card specifies its damage element without a numerical output. Unspecified crit/proc eligibility retains the existing direct-packet defaults and is labeled Partial. Magic Guard was left unsupported because its numeric scaling is not provided.
- Tagged finishers are supported; existing M2 packets have that tag. Unclassified M1 finishers and qualifying ground-slam detection are not inferred. Golem Guillotine’s separate activation preview assumes an eligible slam.
- Royal Parry requires the selected Guaranteed Crit buff. Its attribution if natural crit chance was already 100% remains manual. Chance-based crits and perk-only guaranteed crit do not enable its multiplier.
- Channeled Depths buildup/cap progression and Carrying Winds’ tentative Air-based crit formula remain unimplemented.

## Supporting fixes

The legacy Ferocious enchantment now shares Ferocity’s harmonic Tenacity formula. Fractional bonuses retain precision instead of being truncated to two decimals, and negative fractional Tenacity contributes zero instead of producing a nonfinite value. Whole-number and zero Tenacity remain finite; very large manually entered values use a bounded-work harmonic approximation. Received fractional Shatter penetration retains four decimal places rather than losing a tenth through floating-point truncation.
