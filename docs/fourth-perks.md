# Fourth forty-perk batch

Exactly forty previously Unsupported source-card definitions now have calculations, shared selected-status behavior, or separate activation previews. All forty remain Partial where the game requires timing, healing, summons, charge tracking, stance replacement, or unverified formulas. These are forty new calculated portions, not forty complete combat simulations.

Definitions remain in the existing `src/data` files. No raw runtime catalog or ReviewedCatalog layer was added. Existing build data and earlier perk work are preserved.

## Interactions and manual controls

- Select Bulwark Qualifies after checking its stun threshold. Glyph Conduit and Magic Reinforce are real selected game buffs; their numeric self-preview uses the equipped perk. Tongue Shot Ready, Icestorm Active, Guiding Winds Charged, and Extinguish Proc are scenario/neutral controls and do not increase game-buff counts.
- Friendship, Converted Energy, Cinder Surge, Cloudpush, Cinderpull, and Redirect use their selected applicant, captured innate source, and effective potency. Recipients do not need the originating perk. Final manual potency overrides remain final. Remove and re-add Cinder Surge to capture aura reapplication when the applicant’s Burn state changes.
- Extinguish consumes Burn from any applicant before Curse Rip. Its selected hit requires pre-conversion Water output and a nonzero proc coefficient. Only the Water portion of the first hit gains its bonus. Later hits see the consumed Burn removed. Qualification for Water introduced only by later output conversions is not inferred.
- Forgotten Chivalry consumes carried Reinforce after the triggering positive Rune/Weapon Art hit. The Holy output is limited to that first hit; later equal hits no longer receive it. For first-hit buff-count effects, Reinforce remains present as an explicit ordering convention. Neither calculation mutates the saved build/status selection.
- True Moon converts weapon Magic after other additive outputs and Spirit Winds. This cross-conversion ordering is a builder convention. Cursed Flames changes only its applicant’s Burn profile and stacks its weakening multiplicatively with ordinary Weakness. Ghastly Rot converts Poison on the marked recipient from any applicant, preserving its scaling and the sum of output coefficients.
- Grandmagic Guard applies its diminishing reduction and then flat Magic-family reduction per output and per hit, after reviewed defense/reduction multipliers. Each normal/critical branch floors at zero before expected damage is recomputed. Per-output placement and ordering relative to special vulnerabilities are documented builder conventions. Flat reductions display as damage amounts rather than percentages.
- Apollo Boost adds a fixed two seconds before reviewed CDR/race factors. Friendship contributes its potency-based rate to the existing grouped denominator. The explicit 50%-per-0.1 coefficient/formula is used; the card’s 0.3-potency arithmetic example is inconsistent.
- Pyre Bloom uses the selected Weapon Art’s base cooldown, so Friendship does not alter its fireball base damage. Magic Circuit also uses base Rune cooldown as an explicit convention pending verification. The separate activation panels do not add proc totals to weapon/skill totals.
- Dynamic activation profiles support Propelling Fun’s Fire/Air variant and Essence Ray’s inherited weapon scaling without changing the saved weapon profile. Essence Ray currently has a separate activation preview; its parent Magical Ray panel is not replaced.
- Penance only calculates the verified maximum at HP <= 35%; its above-threshold curve is not invented. Stone Weapon’s probable attack-speed penalty, Guiding Winds’ intermediate charge, and Grandmagic Guard’s transferred potency are held. The remaining per-perk limitations are listed above.

## Additional primary-source checks

The copied board’s potency-stat cards state that each listed perk contributes its amount to the additive DoT potency stat. This batch applies that separate stat contribution for Cursed Flames, Cinder Soul, Pyre Bloom, Sharp Claws, and Spore Burst; innate source potency remains separate. Cursed and Perk Effectiveness now leave direct gear Bleed/Burn/Poison potency unchanged, increasing only the contributing source perks instead of multiplying those gear points a second time. Other potency types retain their previous behavior. The source formula card also explicitly places flat damage reduction after defense.
