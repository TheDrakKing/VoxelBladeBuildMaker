# Fifth forty-perk batch

Forty previously Unsupported source cards now have calculated portions: weapon construction and packet modifiers, speed factors, separate activation previews, or selected shared-status/potency behavior. All forty remain Partial where the board omits formulas or the builder requires manual timing. This does not mean forty fully simulated game effects.

Definitions stay in the existing src/data tables. No raw runtime catalog layer was introduced.

## Weapon and speed ordering

- Construction hooks operate on fresh copies after existing stance resolution. Ascending builder priorities: Artillery Mage 10, Kama Blades 20, Saw Heart 30, Lance 40, Cosmic Ray 60, Mine 70, Steam Charge 100. Kama overriding Dual Wielding is documented by the source. Other conflicting replacement priorities are conventions pending in-game verification.
- Pursuit modifies generated M1 packets at priority 150; Rotating Blades at 200 doubles actual hits and scales per-hit base/proc coefficient once. Steam Charge retains its Fire/Air profile, and normal LMB retains its weapon profile. Independent activation previews are not automatically doubled by Rotating Blades.
- Berserking Strength uses the resolved weapon family, including Dual Unbalanced Swords. Lance/Saw/Gun conversions are not assumed eligible. Snapshot family metadata lets the same qualification govern damage and displayed attack speed.
- Speed starts with average part speed + AttackSpeed / 100 + race flat speed. Berserking and Inhuman factors then multiply that numeric speed; factor placement is a builder convention. Inhuman cap is +300% and denominator >=7 perks is unresolved, so no bonus is invented in that range.
- Artillery uses the specific 5.5 LMB card instead of the general construction card's conflicting 5. Its RMB is held unchanged. Cosmic Ray uses the specific 18.5 instead of the general card's 14. Mine uses the general formula card's 5/10/15 stages, retaining that card's warning about outdated bases. Lance RMB is held because charge and hit count are not exact.

## Selected states and ownership

- Steam Buildup Ready, Finisher Proc Number, Quicksand Weapon Art Tick, Poison via Pure Rot, Parried Hit, Future Sight Ready, Sticky: Sickness Variant, and Draconic Color controls are neutral/scenario qualifiers. They do not add a game buff for Emotional/Ork conditions. Lithic Veil and Minion Absorbed are real buffs. Anti-Venom is explicitly neutral in the source.
- Minion Absorbed snapshots applicant Summon Boost and perk amount on application. The recipient can use it without owning the perk. Existing Bastion and final override semantics apply. Cursed/Perk Effectiveness exclude Minion Absorption, as its card specifies. Negative computed source potency floors at zero.
- Blood Rush offers a summon Rage source; it never automatically buffs the player. The existing second build actor can represent a summon. Summon HP thresholds, AI, and simultaneous minion combat are not simulated. Winter Woof and Gravitational Enforcer offer their documented Shatter sources.
- Cauterize changes only its actual applicant's selected Burn ticks on enemies to zero, keeping the Burn identity for related effects. Its separate burst follows the linked additive Burn Potency formula. Pure Rot requires its explicit source qualifier; kill-credit/transfer attribution remains manual.
- Sickness uses one existing Sticky debuff plus a neutral variant qualifier. The variant adds Hex vulnerability without a duplicate Sticky debuff or extra count. Anti-Venom is a direct x0.1 Hex damage factor after reviewed defense; it also adds 20 Movement Speed points. Its Poison duration extension remains manual.
- Parried Hit is manually qualified and excludes status/DoT packets. Future Sight Ready also excludes status/DoT packets. Neither auto-consumes; split a multi-hit attack and remove Future Sight after the first dodged hit to model the one-use rule.
- Shared Lithic Veil transfer potency is unknown, so only the selected perk-holder self-preview is calculated. Physical output receives 0.2 flat reduction per perk after defense, per hit/output. No guessed Dexterity/Summon damage-type family mapping is introduced.
- Draconic Color normal selection is mutually exclusive. Malformed saves with multiple colors get no Rune bonus. Finisher Proc Number is a sequence counter, not an extra hit multiplier. Reset/skip rules are manual.

## Activation limits

- Caci Spirit uses separate seven-thorn and three-slam segments (5x7 and 6x3), preserving each segment's per-hit damage and flat reduction. Buni assumes all 20 hits hit one selected target; its source says every hit is an RMB finisher. Bomber and Caci Weapon/M2/Finisher classification is explicitly conventional pending game verification. Bowldur is a separate one-hit active-spirit preview, not a full ordinary-weapon replacement.
- Gale Bursts follows the explicit 2.25 + 3.75p formula rather than its inconsistent example. Lodestone, Tectonic Heat, Ice Burst, Gravitational Enforcer, and Thorns have inferred output splits/types, explicitly recorded in their support details. Unverified chances, cooldowns, target counts, and proc inheritance are not folded into DPS.
- Caustic Slow and Virulent Core currently contribute only the source-verified additive Poison potency; their missing Slow-DoT and weapon-blast formulas remain unsupported portions. Splinter similarly contributes additive Bleed without inventing innate application potency. These are counted as Partial.
