## Shared conventions

- Explicit damage snapshots avoid reapplying their source hit's offensive scaling. Derived-hit values in Other Perk Effects are pre-modifier previews, not extra automatic hits.
- Missing base/scaling/output coefficients remain held instead of assuming a 1.0 scaling.
- Newly covered Poison potency grants use the +0.1-per-perk convention requested for Venom Eater/Blood Thirsty; Trello does not numerically specify those grants.
- Bomber Charge maps Retaliate's equal Physical/True split to equal Holy/True. Sandy Wounds transfers the existing Bleed scaling coefficients 1:1. These are explicit builder conventions pending game tests.
- Fractional counters floor, except Delta Drill where the source explicitly specifies rounding.
- Neutral scenario selectors do not count as buffs for buff-dependent perks.
- Spiritual Energy timelines, summon actors, hitboxes, and random proc chains are not simulated.
